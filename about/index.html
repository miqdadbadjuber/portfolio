About Me.

I'm an AI & Machine Learning Engineer based in Indonesia. My primary focus currently centers on training machine learning models and developing specialized agentic coding skills. Alongside model training, I actively experiment with full-stack web applications and AI agent CLI tools, built both independently and for practical technical project submissions.

My journey began in early 2024. At the time, I frequently followed tech influencers discussing the rapid evolution of artificial intelligence, which inspired me to buy the book "The Coming Wave" by Mustafa Suleyman. The book's central warning, describing AI as a double-edged sword that will divide those who adapt from those who fall behind, gave me a strong push to start learning programming that very day.

When I began coding, I used AI as my first mentor, constantly questioning the reasoning and data flow behind every line of logic. That curiosity led me to explore technical deep dives on YouTube, where I discovered the mathematical and algorithmic depth of Machine Learning and Deep Learning, prompting me to enroll in structured technical courses.

Between 2025 and 2026, my learning shifted into hands-on implementation. I subscribed to various AI platforms to examine their capabilities in depth and began building real machine learning projects from scratch. During one rigorous project submission, my work was rejected three times in a row. Instead of quitting, I evaluated every error, restructured the data pipeline, and refined the model architecture until it successfully passed, earning verified credentials from Dicoding Academy, Google Developer recognitions, and dozens of badges from Google Skills.

Today, I channel this experience into building practical projects such as AntiSlop, ContextForge, DiagramPilot, OpenFolio, as well as developing custom AI agent skills deployed within cloud code plugin ecosystems.

I recognize that this field moves quickly and I am continuously learning every day. My focus remains grounded in consistency, genuine curiosity, and a commitment to solving real problems through clean code.
